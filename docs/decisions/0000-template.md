# NNNN: Title in a few words

Copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md` with the next free number. Keep the record
short; it explains one decision to someone who arrives later.

## Context

What problem or constraint forced a choice. Facts, not history.

## Decision

What we do now, stated as a rule.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and which docs or tests enforce it.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative in one or two sentences, and why it lost.
